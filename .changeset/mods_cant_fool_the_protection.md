# Mods can no longer talk you into giving up your account

OneClient now asks before a game switches to another of your accounts, makes changes to your Realm, or opens a link in your browser (once per site). It never opens Microsoft sign-in or device-code pages for a game. A mod can't write its own text into these questions. Once you refuse, it can't keep asking you again. You're told when a mod changes your skin or cape, tries to rename your account, or signs in to servers unusually often. Folders that hold browser data or other logins, such as your user folder or AppData, can no longer be shared with a game.
